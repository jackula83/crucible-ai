- (decision) OQ-3a resolved — errored-run verdict: three-zone rule. passed>=ceil(t*runs) → green; passed+errored<ceil(t*runs) → red (semantic fail); else inconclusive → test fails reported as ERRORED (not failed) with infra causes named.
